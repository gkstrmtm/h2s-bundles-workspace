Pilot program terms

These terms describe the initial customer-benefit pilot. They do not represent government, regulatory, brokerage or legal approval or endorsement.

1. Customer-benefit program

The Move-In Advantage pilot allows approved partners to share optional Home2Smart service benefits with their clients. Participation does not create an employment, agency, franchise or joint-venture relationship with Home2Smart.

2. No partner referral compensation

The pilot does not provide partners with commissions, cash, gift cards, percentage payments or other performance-based compensation for referrals. Attribution may be recorded for service continuity, reporting and program evaluation only.

3. Independent post-closing services

Home2Smart is an independent provider of optional technology installation services. Clients choose whether to contact or hire Home2Smart, contract directly with Home2Smart and may select another provider. Services are not required for a real-estate transaction and are intended to occur after closing or move-in authorization.

4. Brokerage approval

Partners must comply with their brokerage, team and broker-in-charge policies. Home2Smart approval does not replace any consent, disclosure or authorization required by a partner’s supervising organization.

5. Customer discounts and scheduling

Preferred pricing, promotional discounts and priority scheduling are subject to eligibility, service-area coverage, technician availability, property authorization and any offer-specific restrictions. Benefits may be changed, paused or withdrawn.

6. Future program features

No future compensation feature is currently enabled or promised. Any such feature would require separate business, legal and compliance review, new written terms and any necessary brokerage approval before activation.
