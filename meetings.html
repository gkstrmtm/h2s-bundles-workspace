❓ How to Use the Meeting Scheduler

📅 Meeting Tracker

  • Select your sales area from the dropdown to view upcoming meetings
  • View the next meeting with countdown timer
  • Add meetings to your calendar with one click
  • Subscribe to get email notifications for new meetings

🔐 Manager Dashboard

  • Login with your PIN to access manager features
  • Create, edit, and delete meetings
  • Meetings automatically create Google Calendar events
  • Published meetings send email notifications to subscribers
  • Schedule reminders to notify subscribers before meetings

💡 Talking Points

  • View current talking points for your area
  • Points are automatically filtered by date range
  • Updated weekly with the latest information
  • Managers: Create and manage talking points for all areas

📧 Email Notifications

  • Subscribe to receive meeting notifications via email
  • Notifications sent when new meetings are published
  • Optional reminder notifications before meetings
