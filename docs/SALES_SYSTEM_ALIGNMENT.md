# Home2Smart Sales System Alignment

Audit date: November 4, 2026. Development baseline: `retro-dev` at `0176651f6bfb43c4050b2773bc4d697746ffaf39`.

## Authority and conclusion

`Home2Smart_Lean_Sales_System_Specification.pdf` was unavailable after workspace searches, including ignored files. The supplied Comprehensive System Alignment & Execution Audit requirements are the authoritative working summary.

This document, the [execution plan](SALES_SYSTEM_EXECUTION_PLAN.md), and the [progress tracker](SALES_SYSTEM_PROGRESS.md) govern **remaining development**. Earlier numbered-phase reports and the [remote review](REMOTE_DEVELOPMENT_REVIEW.md) remain historical inventories; their full-payment-first recommendation and numbered next-phase recommendations are superseded.

**We have reusable foundations, not a production-ready sales operation.** The dashboard, owned prospects, assessments, server-authoritative quotes, exception approvals, customer agreement page, test-only payment reconciliation, calendar, preparation and managed offers should remain. The missing work is their connection to the specified 25% deposit -> cancellation clearance -> installation -> 75% balance -> actual-cost commission -> Friday settlement journey.

Five representatives means five pilot accounts, **not** a database headcount cap. Management reports no known active customer installations needing migration; this is a supplied operating assumption, not a new production-data verification. Historical Dispatch records must remain separately labeled legacy evidence, not automatically become active installations.

## Evidence boundaries

- Actual saved source, routes, SQL contracts, pricing calculations and tests were inspected, alongside the three existing review documents. Financial conclusions include a separate trace of legacy technician payout creation, approval and withdrawal endpoints.
- **Implemented/tested** below means isolated automated behavior or source-backed contracts. It does not certify deployed schema, grants, storage, credentials, current catalog publication, real Stripe events or bank settlement.
- **Inactive** means implemented behind development/production gates; no gates were activated.
- **Preview-only** means the actual frontend running against synthetic responses. It is not evidence of production authentication, payment, scheduling writes or fulfillment.
- **Unverified** dependencies include actual Supabase project co-location, native database permissions/triggers, private storage, Stripe test-account integration and legal approval. No production records or credentials were inspected.
- **Incomplete** means a required integrated contract, UI or policy does not yet exist. Local dirty work and unsaved editor buffers were not overwritten or treated as committed evidence.

## Pricing and payout corrections that matter

1. [home2smart.v2.json](../config/pricing/home2smart.v2.json#L20) is proposed, activation-disabled, with estimated 3% processing and 5% reserve. Its notes explicitly model commission after reserve on positive contribution. That is **not** the specified operational base. Do not publish it or reuse its economics unchanged.
2. [Reconciliation arithmetic](../config/pricing/reconciliation/reconcile.cjs#L194) and its [wage-exclusion regression](../config/pricing/reconciliation/reconcile.test.cjs#L145) are useful validation patterns. Actual earning must not silently import illustrative percentages, estimated costs, reserves, minimum-margin rules or positive-only floors. Negative/unknown bases need explicit policy review.
3. The public storefront reads active database bundles/services; the proposed comprehensive JSON and prepared drafts are not proof of current payable prices. Freeze an explicitly approved launch catalog, including exclusions/qualification conditions, before real transactions.
4. Existing [35% technician compensation](../backend/lib/dataOrchestration.ts#L518) is not a salesperson commission. Do not stack it into, replace it with, or deduct wages from the 55/50/45 base. Track actual job time and technician compensation separately for company economics.
5. Existing payment locks, current-net balance calculation and management exception approval already work in isolated tests. These are integration assets, not absent features to rebuild.

## Fulfillment risks and historical boundaries

- [Managed assignment](../backend/migrations/041_management_assignment.sql#L165) and [atomic portal responses](../backend/migrations/042_management_offer_reliability.sql#L96) should serve new enrolled orders. Acceptance assigns; decline requeues. Pending offers and calendar reservations remain distinct availability evidence.
- Reviewed Dispatch-to-legacy links require verified IDs, not names/email ([042](../backend/migrations/042_management_offer_reliability.sql#L4)). Current offer creation does not require every historical payout bridge to be resolved. Blanket historical reconciliation must not block unrelated sales development.
- New-order fulfillment needs a verified canonical technician/job relationship for completion and technician compensation. Establish it for **new enrolled jobs only**; do not manufacture historical jobs or silently match legacy email identities.
- [Legacy completion rollback](../backend/app/api/portal_mark_done/route.ts#L271) attempts `scheduled`, which the [Dispatch CHECK](../backend/migrations/002_fix_job_status_constraint.sql#L13) excludes. Completion and payout side effects are nontransactional; failure must not claim rollback succeeded.
- [Existing commercial guards](../backend/migrations/036_commercial_calendar_workload.sql#L419) restrict linked completion and require readiness/reservations before starting. A compatible, explicitly scoped completion authority is required, not bypassing those guards.
- [Legacy reschedule](../backend/app/api/customer_reschedule/route.ts#L203) also writes `scheduled`. Keep the new path on established transactional reservation authority; do not route new orders through this defective fallback or undertake an unrelated historical rewrite.
- No blanket assignment activation now. Future activation must be scoped to certified new orders and explicit authorization. Historical issues remain a separate investigation queue.

## Preserve, simplify, defer

**Preserve:** [frontend/dash.html](../frontend/dash.html) as the management foundation; existing authentication/customer identities, configurable assessments/private photos, quote revisions/approvals, agreement page, Stripe authority, Operations, company calendar, reservation/preparation logic, managed portal responses, legacy checkout and technician behavior.

**Simplify:** One management **Orders** work queue within existing Field Sales/Operations, linked by existing lead/quote/order/job IDs. Default new-sales operational counts to explicitly enrolled orders, not historical Dispatch statuses. Keep rep quote/agreement actions embedded in the current workspace; add a clear next action and compact orders/performance/earnings view. Reuse intake/address/service data across quote and scheduling. No duplicate customer entry, approval queue, calendar or mandatory trip through legacy Dispatch.

**Defer:** Broad dashboard-IIFE refactors, wholesale legacy repair, automatic dispatch optimization, offline/native apps, expanded roles, new CRM/calendar/payment/payroll subscriptions, email notifications, additional offers and the unapproved bonus. Legacy Dispatch remains comparison/fallback; its mobile clipping need not hold the pilot if the consolidated job path is complete and usable.

These navigation recommendations follow the retrieved Exhibit `internal-operations` guidance: operational-compact, border-first, one dominant task surface, explicit task/state labels and minimal CTAs. No UI/CSS changes were made.

### Legal evidence, not legal approval

The directly inspected [South Carolina statute, sections 37-2-501 through 505](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t37c002.php) defines the covered home-solicitation context, provides a third-business-day cancellation rule and requires a signed writing/customer copy. Section 503 also preserves cancellation rights until compliance. Applicability to the particular credit/card/service transaction must be reviewed; do not assume every transaction has the same clock or exceptions.

The [DOL outside-sales fact sheet](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/17f-overtime-outside-sales) requires the actual duties and away-from-business work tests; a SALES title alone establishes no exemption or contractor classification.

Counsel should also verify the [FTC Cooling-Off Rule](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/cooling-off-rule-when-and-how-cancel-sale) and [16 CFR 429](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-429). Direct FTC retrieval returned 403 and eCFR redirected to an automated-access challenge; their full current text was not independently retrieved in this audit. No legal terms, blanket three-day deadline or exemption was approved.
